How I work

My process from first question to final handoff.

Listen

Talk to users, read the data, and write down the real problem in one sentence.

Sketch

Draw many rough ideas fast, then pick the clearest flow with the team.

Build

Turn it into polished screens and a prototype that feels like the real thing.

Test and fix

Watch people use it, note where they stumble, and revise until they glide.
